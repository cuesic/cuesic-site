Cuesic Mobile App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy covers the Cuesic mobile app. For our general website privacy policy, see our website Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating your account, including your name, username, and email address. We also collect data about your use of the app such as events you attend and artists you follow.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the app, personalise your experience, and communicate with you about events and features.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. Your profile information (name, username, profile picture) is visible to other Cuesic users. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the app.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely via Supabase. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile settings. Upon deletion, your personal data will be removed from our systems.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at app@cuesic.com
